## 1. Title Pattern
Tool-name plus concrete function and target domain. This is a common ICCAD style when the paper centers on a named engine.

## 2. Abstract Move Order
1. practical bottleneck
2. specialized method/tool
3. what makes it effective
4. main comparative result
5. venue-style calibration value

## 3. Introduction Framing
Likely very direct, with little background padding and a strong emphasis on the concrete flow bottleneck.

## 4. Method Narration
Useful for learning concise algorithm-paper narration in modern ICCAD style.

## 5. Experiment Narration
Expect strong comparison tables and benchmark evidence with less architectural exposition than placement papers.

## 6. Wording Habits
Helpful for learning recent ICCAD verbs such as comprehensive, effective, and scalable, but use them only with proof nearby.

## 7. Figure And Table Logic
May use fewer architecture figures and rely more on flow or partition illustrations plus tables.

## 8. Reusable Lessons For Our Paper
- imitate: Use it for venue-tone calibration.
- avoid: Avoid letting this more tool-centric style dominate your accelerator-specific placement narrative.
