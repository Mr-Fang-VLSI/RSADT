## One-Paragraph Abstract (rewritten)
RippleFPGA focuses on routability-driven placement for large heterogeneous FPGAs. It proposes a multi-stage flow that includes partitioning, packing, congestion estimation, global placement, legalization, and routing-resource-aware detailed placement, demonstrating strong routability on ISPD2016 benchmarks.

## Problem and Context
As FPGA designs grow, congestion and routing-resource limits become dominant placement issues. Purely wirelength-driven analytical placers may miss FPGA-specific routability failures in large heterogeneous designs.

## Method
- key idea: Use a staged routability-driven FPGA placement framework with explicit congestion modeling and routing-resource-aware detailed refinement.
- assumptions: Assumes large heterogeneous FPGAs where congestion is a first-class objective and where FPGA architecture legality matters throughout the flow.
- model/formula: No single compact model dominates the extracted text; the novelty lies in the staged congestion-aware flow.
