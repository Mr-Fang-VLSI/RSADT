# Writing Landscape For ICCAD-Style FPGA Placement Papers

## Stable Traits Across The Corpus
1. The title is usually direct, technical, and low-drama.
2. The abstract usually names the bottleneck in the first two sentences.
3. Contributions are often bulletized in the introduction.
4. Method sections are usually divided into explicit stages or modules.
5. Primary quantitative results appear early in the results section.
6. The best papers keep strong claims close to concrete metrics.

## What The Current Paper Should Emulate
1. `Systolic Array Placement on FPGAs` for domain-specific problem framing.
2. `SysMix` for sequel-paper contribution ordering.
3. `Timing-Driven Placement for FPGAs with Heterogeneous Architectures and Clock Constraints` for timing-plus-constraint method narration.
4. `An Effective Timing-Driven Detailed Placement Algorithm for FPGAs` for explaining why timing-aware optimization is needed beyond simple weighting.

## Drafting Recommendation
When the method story is ready, draft in this order:
1. title candidates
2. abstract beats
3. introduction with contribution bullets
4. method overview figure caption and section skeleton
5. experimental setup
6. result table captions and claims
7. conclusion
